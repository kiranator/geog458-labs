# Lab 01: My Digital Geography Workspace

## Place of interest
New York, New York

## Geographic question
Does race affect access to reliable public transportation?

## Possible data
Neighbourhood boundaries, race and ethnicity data, subway station and bus stop locations, frequency and on-time performance data, travel time

## Mapping idea
Use a web map to layer transit access (measured by distance to nearest subway station, as well as service frequency) over neighbourhood-level racial demographics
